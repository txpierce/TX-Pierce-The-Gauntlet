Complete 75-Minute Program Itinerary & Stations

0:00 – 0:12 | Dynamic Warm-Up & Movement Prep

Light jogs, high knees, butt kicks, karaoke, lateral shuffles, World's Greatest Stretch, spiderman crawls, leg swings, inchworms, pogo hops, build-up sprints, and deceleration stops.

0:12 – 0:18 | Rules Brief & Partner Pairings

Home plate huddle establishing the 45-minute master clock, locking in head-to-head partner matchups, and reviewing mental error penalties.

0:18 – 1:03 | The Baseball Gauntlet (45-Minute Master Clock)

Clockwise rotation (15s sprint transitions). Round 1 (Base: 2:00/stn + 3m break) → Round 2 (Tempo: 1:30/stn + 3m break) → Round 3 (Perfection: 1:00/stn, zero-defect standard).

Station 1: Reactive Mirror & 10-Yard First-Step Shuttles

Partners face in low fielding crouch. Partner A shuffles 5 yds, touches line, sprints 10 yds through finish cone; Partner B mirrors and races.

Station 2: Rotational Med-Ball Shot-Put & Scoop

8–10 lb medicine ball against brick wall/fence. Explode from back hip into rotational throw, field rebound cleanly, fire from opposite side.

Station 3: 60-Foot Steal Starts & Deceleration Box

Primary/secondary lead. Execute sharp crossover steal start on movement, sprint 60 ft, and come to complete under-control stop inside 10-ft cone box.

Station 4: Rapid-Fire Barehand Short-Hops

Partners kneel/squat 10 ft apart with 1 baseball (no gloves). Rapidly roll/bounce short-hops, funneling to midline with soft hands. Any drop resets count to zero.

Station 5: Lateral Hurdle Hops & 15-Yard Burst

Three 6-inch mini-hurdles. Rapid double-leg lateral hops over all three, stick final landing, open hips into 15-yard sprint.

Station 6: Heavy Farmer's Carry

40–50 lb dumbbells/kettlebells (30–35 lbs for freshmen/JV). Carry down and back along 20-yard lane with tall posture and packed shoulders.

1:03 – 1:08 | Championship Finisher: Team Line Drill

2 sets of 17s (foul line to foul line or 60-yd shuttles). Strict target time (under 32s). Team finishes together or runs extra.

1:08 – 1:15 | Cool-Down, Breathing & Accountability Debrief

Down-regulation walk, static hip flexor/hamstring stretches, and team huddle shoutouts for leadership and clean footwork.
